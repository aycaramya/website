The first time I read the artist's way by julia cameron, it ended with me writing an EP and performing some of the songs with a band. I tried rereading it a couple years later but couldn't stick with it, and then couldn't stick with it again a few months later. I guess something clicked recently cause I'm on my 4th attempt and I decided to make this page halfway through week 1.


I've been asked a few times what my album writing process looks like, and I've never really known how to accurately describe it, so this is my latest attempt to try and do so.


I don't really like julia cameron's writing style but her book also changed my life and both of those things can be true.
